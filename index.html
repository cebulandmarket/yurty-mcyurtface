The Space

Escape to a hidden paradise

You're staying in a cozy yurt in the forest. A stream runs just outside, creating a peaceful atmosphere after a day of trekking nearby. From the yurt, wake up to a beautiful view of the famous Seven Bulls Rock.

We provide yoga mats for meditation, binoculars, and a guitar for music 🎶 — and breakfast is on us, every morning.

Whether you're here to unwind or explore, every moment is an invitation to experience the beauty of the wilderness. Stretch out by the stream, borrow the binoculars for an up-close look at nature's wonders, or feel inspired to play a tune while basking in the scenery.

Private patio, fully-fenced private backyard, and an indoor wood-burning fireplace to keep you warm through Kyrgyz winters.

Yoga mats, binoculars, and a guitar are all here waiting — plus a free home-cooked breakfast served at the guest house every morning.

What To Do

Trek, ride, or simply do nothing

For the adventurous, embark on a roughly 4-hour trek to a stunning waterfall tucked away in the forest.

Craving more? Hop on a horseback ride and experience the landscape like never before — lush meadows, rugged red cliffs, and endless views of the Seven Bulls.

Or stay close: stretch out on the yoga mats by the stream, meditate with the mountains in front of you, and let the sound of sheep and cow bells be the only noise all day.

Our Story

How this yurt came to be

Clayton and I are a travelling couple, and we stayed at the guest house with the family. We had a fantastic connection with them from the first day. We lived there for weeks, and we volunteered at the school in the village. We gave supplies and a printer to the small school. We went horse riding. We went to the cave and walked out to the river.

We have so much love for the small community. We walked out to where the yurt is located. It was forest, and you could hear animals in the distance. The birds, the water stream, the horses, the sheep. I loved how we were far from the neighbourhood but still close to the community and the land. Close enough to nature to really feel it. A wide sky, where you feel small under millions of stars above us.

Clayton and I decided to put a yurt here and live a couple of months. The family and Cholpon helped us with everything we needed. We lived at the yurt for 2 months during winter. It was the best remote life we ever had. We never feel bored, and that is the best thing about this place. You get to be busy, but in a slowed-down life. Waking up to the beautiful snow outside, with the beautiful view of the Bull Rock.

We have so much love for this yurt. We hid little secrets all around it. Clayton loves adding tiny special touches, his own way of expressing himself. We did yoga and meditated in front of the fireplace, feeling the warmth it gives our body. Connected, and alive.

Something of that winter stayed in the yurt. We hope you feel it too.
